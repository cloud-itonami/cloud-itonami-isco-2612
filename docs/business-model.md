# Business Model: Independent Arbitration & Adjudication Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-2612`
- ISCO-08: `2612`
- Occupation: Judges
- Social impact: access-to-justice, dispute-resolution-efficiency, local-jobs

## Customer

- disputing parties (commercial or civil)
- law firms seeking private adjudication
- arbitration institutions

## Offer

- binding arbitration
- non-binding mediation
- case-file and hearing-record management

## Revenue

- per-case arbitration fee
- hourly mediation fee
- hearing-record management fee

## Trust Controls

- no binding award issuance without governor gate
- recusal/conflict-of-interest disclosure required before accepting a case
- hearing records are auditable, not editable
